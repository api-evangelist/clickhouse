This article demonstrates how to instrument two ASP.NET services with OpenTelemetry and ship observability data to ClickStack, achieving cross-service execution view by combining traces, logs, and metrics. The tutorial shows developers how to follow requests end-to-end across service boundaries and database operations without manual correlation.
